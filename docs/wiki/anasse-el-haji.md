# Anasse El Haji

**Résumé.** Owner of the Personal workspace and of the Fisca project, working in French.

## Texte complet

Anasse El Haji owns this workspace and the [[fisca]] project, a Moroccan tax and invoicing SaaS. He works in French, gives agents detailed specification documents to configure, and personally arbitrates the spec questions the agents raise. [1]

## How to work with him
- Reply in French and write deliverables (prompts, docs, rubrics) in French unless he asks otherwise. [2]
- He wants to reduce costs on the agent factory, but a cheaper model is adopted only after a qualification test in the project; he asks for cost estimates (about 331 $ spent by 2026-11-14) and is considering moving the build loop to the Antigravity CLI. [3][4][5]
- He expects the autonomous build to run in a loop, and wants to be shown how to launch it; after wave 2 he asked the pilot to stop until he authorises wave 3 explicitly. [6][7]

## Involved in now
- [[fisca-software-factory]]: owner of the agent team that builds Fisca. He supplied the 18 specs (now in the [[fisca-monorepo]]), validates the skill, rubric and agent cards, protects main, renews the GitHub token, and decides when each wave starts. [6][8]
- His arbitrations are recorded in the repo's ERRATA (D-22, D-23) and summarised on [[fisca-engineering-rules]], [[fisca-pos-rules]] and [[fisca-chart-of-accounts]]. [9]

## Uses agents for
Configuring projects from documents, building Fisca with specialised agents; the workspace also has marketing, video and analysis agents.
