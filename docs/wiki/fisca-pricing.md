# Fisca pricing

**Résumé.** The price grids for Fisca's direct, accounting-firm and large-account offers.

## Texte complet

All prices in MAD, excluding VAT (HT). The public site shows HT prices only, since TTC would need a hard-coded VAT rate. [1]

## Direct offers (self-employed, TPE, PME)
- Solo AE: 129 MAD/month. [2]
- Essentiel TPE: 390 MAD/month. [2]
- Croissance PME: 890 MAD/month. [2]
- 14-day Sandbox trial and 2 months free per year. [2]
- Pack TPME Mowakaba: 18,000 MAD HT. [2]

## Accounting firms (fiduciaire), metered
- Base 490 MAD/month plus a price per active client: 69 MAD (Simplifié), 149 MAD (TPE) or 249 MAD (PME Complet). [3]
- Prorata is exact and daily, in integer cents (activeDays/daysInMonth), with no minimum month billed and a floor of 1 day if a client is activated and deactivated the same day (owner decision, 2026-11-14). [4]
- 15% discount from 100 files. [3]
- Pack Mowakaba Cabinet: 45,000 MAD HT. [3]

## Large accounts (revenue above 200M MAD, excluding Mowakaba), annual contract
- Corporate: 58,000 MAD HT/year, 5 ICE. [5]
- Fisca Souverain Loi 05-20: 140,000 MAD HT/year, 10 ICE, GPU and database in Morocco. [5]
