# Curation wiki : un flag d'ambiguïté doit contenir six éléments

> Remarque d'export : règle propre à l'agent Fisca-Wiki-Memory-Curator (voir note ci-dessus).

## Contenu intégral (inchangé)

When curating Fisca Wiki memories, flagging an ambiguity is not enough. Judges penalize flags that 'do not give' the information needed to act. Every flag or escalation must include all six of these parts:
1. WHAT is ambiguous: quote the exact conflicting or unclear text, with its source or page.
2. WHY it matters: the concrete risk if it is guessed wrong, such as a wrong tax rate, a stale deadline, a misattributed owner, or a duplicate entry.
3. THE OPTIONS: two or three plausible interpretations.
4. A RECOMMENDED DEFAULT with reasoning, plus a confidence level (high, medium or low).
5. WHAT YOU DID IN THE MEANTIME: for example, stored the entry provisionally with an 'unverified' tag, kept both versions, or withheld the edit.
6. WHO OR WHAT RESOLVES IT: the specific question to ask, and the owner, document or date that would settle it.

Distinguish ambiguities you can resolve yourself from those you must escalate. Resolve them yourself when there is a clear newest or authoritative source, and state the rule you applied. Escalate when the issue is high-stakes, such as fiscal figures, legal or compliance content, or conflicts between authoritative sources.

Never silently pick one interpretation. Never write a vague 'this may need review' with no options or recommendation. Never hedge on everything: unnecessary flags dilute the important ones. Put flags in a clearly labeled section at the top or end of the output so they are not buried.
