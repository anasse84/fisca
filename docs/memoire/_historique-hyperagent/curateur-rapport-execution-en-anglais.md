# Curateur wiki : rapport d'exécution en anglais (règle Hyperagent / juges)

> **Non applicable à Antigravity** — mémoire propre à Hyperagent, conservée pour historique uniquement. Ne pas appliquer ces règles.

## Contenu intégral (inchangé)

In Fisca Wiki Memory Curator tasks, always write the final execution report in English, even when the wiki content, source documents or task instructions are in French. The regression in 'Execution Report Clarity' came from the agent matching the report's language to the French source material, and judges marked the report down for being in French. Separate the two layers. (1) Wiki/memory content: keep it in its original language, usually French, and do not translate curated entries unless asked. (2) Execution report about what you did: write it in English. You may quote French page titles, fiscal terms (e.g., 'TVA', 'impôt sur les sociétés', 'CFE') or entry names verbatim, but add a short English gloss in parentheses on first mention, and keep all explanations, headings, summaries and status lines in English. Before submitting, check that every heading and sentence you wrote yourself is in English and only quoted identifiers remain in French. Use a clear structure: Summary, Actions taken (created/updated/merged/deleted entries with counts), Issues/conflicts found, Skipped items with reasons, Next steps.
